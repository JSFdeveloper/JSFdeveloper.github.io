Delete Your Tree Folio Account

Use this page to request deletion of your Tree Folio account and associated Firebase Authentication account information.

Submit a deletion request

Email us from the address associated with your Tree Folio account. Include “Tree Folio account deletion” in the subject. We may ask you to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

Email an account deletion request

If your email program does not open, email jeremyshootsfilm@outlook.com with the subject “Tree Folio account deletion.”

What is deleted

After we verify and process your request, we delete the Tree Folio Firebase Authentication account and associated account identifiers held by that service. We will confirm when the request is completed.

Your journal and backups

Tree Folio currently stores tree profiles, journal entries, photos, and pot inventory locally on your device; it does not sync them to the account. Deleting your account therefore does not erase those local records. To remove the app’s current local copy, delete your trees and pots in the app or clear Tree Folio’s app storage/uninstall the app. Android Auto Backup copies or exported backup files may remain in the Google Account or storage location you control and must be managed separately.

Processing and contact

We will process verified requests as soon as reasonably possible and contact you if we need information to verify the account. We may retain information where required by law or for legitimate security purposes, as described in the Tree Folio Privacy Policy.
