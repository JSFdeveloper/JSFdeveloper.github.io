Tree Folio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Tree Folio bonsai journal app (“Tree Folio,” “we,” or “the app”) accesses and handles information. Tree Folio is published under the developer name shown on its Google Play listing.

Information stored on your device

Your tree profiles, species selections, journal notes and dates, photos, pot inventory, display preferences, and saved weather location are stored locally on your device. Tree Folio does not currently sync this journal content to a Tree Folio cloud account. Photos selected for journal entries are copied into app storage so they remain available to the app. Android Auto Backup may back up app files and preferences to your Google Account or transfer them between devices, depending on your Android backup settings and device. This operating-system backup is separate from Tree Folio account sync.

If you export a backup, Tree Folio creates a backup file at the location you choose. You control that file and any copies you make. Restoring a backup imports its contents onto your device.

Account information

You may sign in or create an account using email and password or Google Sign-In. Firebase Authentication, a Google service, processes account identifiers such as your email address, authentication provider, and Firebase user identifier to authenticate your account and maintain sign-in. Google Sign-In may also provide your account display name, email address, and profile image to the app; Tree Folio currently uses your first name for optional personalization.

Signing in does not currently upload or sync your tree profiles, journal entries, photos, or pot inventory. Account information is handled by Google under its own privacy terms.

Location and weather

Weather is a Premium feature. If you choose “Use current location,” Tree Folio requests approximate device location only at that time. It does not request background location or continuously track you. You can instead enter a city or postal code. Your selected location is saved on the device for weather display.

To provide weather, the app resolves an entered place to coordinates using the device’s geocoding service, or uses the approximate coordinates you selected, then sends coordinates rounded to four decimal places to the applicable weather provider: the U.S. National Weather Service (NWS) in covered U.S. areas, or MET Norway elsewhere. Google Play services may process the location request on the device. These providers receive the coordinates needed to return the forecast and handle them under their own policies.

Subscriptions and app diagnostics

Premium subscriptions are purchased and managed through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details. Tree Folio receives subscription status to enable Premium features; the app does not receive your full payment card details.

The app includes Firebase Analytics. Google may process app usage events, app instance or device identifiers, and related device or diagnostic information to provide analytics and improve app reliability. Tree Folio does not intentionally send journal text or journal photos through Analytics.

Sharing

Tree Folio does not sell your personal information. Information is handled by service providers needed for the features you use: Google Firebase Authentication and Analytics, Google Play services and Google Play Billing, the device geocoding service, and the NWS or MET Norway weather service. These providers process information as described above and under their own privacy terms.

Retention and deletion

Locally stored journal information remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Android Auto Backup copies and exported backup files are separate and may remain in the Google Account or storage location you control. Deleting your Tree Folio account removes the Firebase Authentication account; because journal content is not uploaded to or linked with that account, account deletion does not automatically erase local journal content or separate backups.

You can request account deletion at any time using the account deletion page linked below. The page explains how to submit a request. We will use the submitted account email to locate and delete the account. We retain no account data longer than needed to complete the request, except where retention is required by law or for security purposes.

Security

Journal content is kept in the app’s private storage on your device. Network requests to sign-in, billing, and weather services use those providers’ supported secure connections. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children’s privacy

Tree Folio is not directed to children and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The current version will be available at this page. For privacy questions or account deletion requests, contact jeremyshootsfilm@outlook.com.
