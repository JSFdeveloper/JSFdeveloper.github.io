Tree Folio Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy describes how Tree Folio, the bonsai journal app, handles information.

Information stored on your device

Tree profiles, species selections, journal notes and dates, photos, pot inventory, preferences, and saved weather location are stored locally on your device. Journal content does not currently sync to a Tree Folio cloud account. Photos are copied into app storage. Android Auto Backup may copy app files and preferences to your Google Account or transfer them between devices according to your Android settings. Exported backups are saved where you choose; you control those files.

Account information

You may sign in with email/password or Google Sign-In. Firebase Authentication, a Google service, processes account identifiers such as email, authentication provider, and Firebase user ID to authenticate you. Google Sign-In may provide your display name, email, and profile image; the app may use your first name for personalization. Signing in does not upload or sync journal content.

Location and weather

Weather is a Premium feature. If you choose “Use current location,” the app requests approximate device location at that time only; it does not request background location or continuously track you. You may instead enter a city or postal code. The selected location is saved on your device. For forecasts, coordinates rounded to four decimal places are sent to the U.S. National Weather Service in covered U.S. areas or MET Norway elsewhere. The device geocoding service or Google Play services may process location information as needed.

Subscriptions and diagnostics

Premium subscriptions are purchased through Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details; Tree Folio receives subscription status to enable features and does not receive your full card details. Firebase Analytics may process app usage events, app/device identifiers, and diagnostics to help improve reliability. Tree Folio does not intentionally send journal text or photos through Analytics.

Sharing and retention

Tree Folio does not sell personal information. Google Firebase Authentication and Analytics, Google Play services and Billing, the device geocoding service, and NWS or MET Norway process information required for the features you use, under their own terms. Local journal information remains until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall. Auto Backup copies and exported files may remain separately. Deleting your account does not erase local journal content or separate backups.

Account deletion

To request deletion of your Firebase Authentication account, follow the instructions on the account deletion page. Account deletion does not delete local journal data. We retain account data only as needed to process the request, except where law or security purposes require otherwise.

Security and children

Journal content is kept in the app’s private device storage. Sign-in, billing, and weather requests use the providers’ supported secure connections. No electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Tree Folio is not directed to children and is not designed to collect children’s personal information.
